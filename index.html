📖 Lore & Elemental Guide

⚡ Turn-Based Combat Rules

AetherQuest is a single-player tactical JRPG inspired by AdventureQuest. On your turn, choose to Attack with your equipped weapon, cast powerful Spells, use consumable Potions/Bombs, or Swap Gear in the middle of battle without forfeiting your turn!

🔥 Elemental Advantage Matrix

  • 🔥 Fire: Incinerates Ice, Nature/Plants, and Undead foes (+50% Dmg). Weak against Water.
  • 💧 Water/Ice: Douses Fire (+60% Dmg) and can freeze swift enemies. Weak to Lightning.
  • 🌿 Earth: Grounds and crushes Lightning (+55% Dmg). Weak to Fire.
  • ⚡ Lightning: Devastates Water and airborne creatures (+60% Dmg). Weak to Earth.
  • ✨ Light: Annihilates Demons, Undead, and Shadows (+75% Dmg!). Countered by Darkness.
  • 🌑 Darkness: Corrupts Mortals and Living (+50% Dmg). Weak to Light.

📈 Progression & Scaling

Every level grants increased base stats plus 5 Stat Points to allocate freely in your Character Sheet (Strength, Dexterity, Intellect, Endurance, Luck). Enemies dynamically scale based on your level and quest tier!
